It's been a while.  

These days, have been enjoying reviving my ancient mid-2014 MBP with [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/), and going rather deep down the **AI**-assisted dev rabbit hole.

Now, there's quite something to like. Yet, felt bit outdated and bloated.  
Especially, given state and ecosystem of latest & greatest LLMs.

Thus, rolled my own :)

Plainly called **[Factory](https://github.com/robi42/factory)**, _fy_ for short.

Bleeding edge, lean, have fun.
